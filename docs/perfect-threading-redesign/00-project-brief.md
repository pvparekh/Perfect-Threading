# Perfect Threading Salon: redesign brief

Date: 2026-10-11

## Mission
Transform the existing Next.js Pages Router website into a distinctive, warm, editorial and practical local-salon experience. Preserve approved content, all prices and offers, reviews, original public routes, Calendly and telephone appointment behavior, and external destinations.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
- Do not modify main, production deployments, booking providers, service prices or approved copy.
- Maintain the exact 56 original service names and prices within five categories.
- Keep all three articles and existing client testimonials intact.
- Keep new content limited to factual information already approved, navigation labels and neutral interaction affordances.
- Preserve existing images; do not misrepresent images as verified photos of the location.
- Target WCAG 2.2 AA, desktop/ultrawide and mobile usability, and strong Core Web Vitals.
- Do not use em dashes in new public writing. Original approved em dashes are preserved.

## Success criteria
The full catalogue is straightforward to scan, all booking routes behave exactly as before, phone and directions remain accessible, pages maintain editorial visual cohesion, and automated and manual tests substantiate quality. A successful build alone is not completion.

## Operational state
Working branch: design/research-led-salon-redesign. Production: main, untouched. Actual deployment preview and screenshot-based verification remain separate gates.
