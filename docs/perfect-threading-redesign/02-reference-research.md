## Decision synthesis
A local salon needs clearer services, price comprehension and appointment routing more than aesthetic gimmicks. Pair the welcoming warmth of neighborhood beauty design with editorial imagery and typography. Reserve complex layouts for image-led storytelling and use extremely legible lists for pricing. Keep the booking interactions unambiguous.

## Customer scenarios
1. New local customer: search to homepage/services, scan offers and prices, then book/call.
2. Returning customer: immediate navigation to Booking and original provider or phone.
3. Researcher: all five service sections expanded and visible, with per-item price alignment.
4. Mobile local-search arrival: quick navigation, readable phone and contact paths, manageable responsive menu.

## Unresolved evidence
Because browser rendering was not available, qualitative conclusions about live animations, color palettes and particular reference website responsiveness are directional, not tested facts.
